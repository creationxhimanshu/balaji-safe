About Company Name

Built on Strength. Driven by Trust.

Company Name is a manufacturer of heavy-duty safes, tijoris and security storage systems. We combine traditional steel craftsmanship with modern engineering to deliver products that protect what matters — for decades.

Our Story

A Legacy of Careful Manufacturing.

Company Name was founded on a simple idea — that security products deserve the same care and attention as the valuables they protect. From our earliest days, we committed to building safes and tijoris that would outlast trends, technologies and even the businesses that bought them.

Our manufacturing philosophy is straightforward: use honest materials, apply proven engineering, test every unit, and stand behind what we make. We do not chase shortcuts. We do not compromise on steel thickness, weld quality or locking precision.

Today, Company Name serves homes, businesses, institutions and government organizations across the country. Our products are found in jewellery showrooms, corporate offices, bank branches, hotels and private residences — wherever dependable security is non-negotiable.

We continue to invest in our factory, our people and our processes. Because security is not a feature — it is a promise.

Description

Product Overview.

The CNS-HD 2400 is Company Name's flagship heavy-duty safe, built for environments where security cannot be compromised. Its double-walled steel body, multi-point locking and anti-drill protection make it suitable for jewellery, cash, important documents and high-value assets.

The door features live locking bolts on three sides, engaged by a precision-machined lock mechanism. Optional configurations include electronic digital locks, mechanical dial locks, or dual-lock arrangements for two-person control.

The interior is fully customizable — adjustable shelves, drawer compartments, document sleeves and carpeted surfaces are available based on requirement.

Security Engineering

Security Engineered Into Every Detail.

Every Company Name product is built around a set of disciplined security principles — from material selection to locking mechanism to final testing.

01 / STRUCTURE

Steel Body Construction

The foundation of every safe is its body. We use thick-gauge cold-rolled steel, cut and formed with precision to eliminate weak points.

  • Heavy-gauge CRCA steel sheets
  • Double-walled body construction
  • Reinforced corner joints
  • Continuous weld seams
02 / DOOR

Reinforced Door Assembly

The door is the most critical component. Ours is built with multiple steel layers, anti-pry lips and precision-machined hinge arrangements.

  • Multi-layer door plate
  • Full-length hinge bar
  • Anti-pry recessed design
  • Sealed gasket option
03 / LOCKING

Multi-Point Locking Systems

Our locking mechanisms engage bolts on multiple sides of the door, distributing force and resisting prying, drilling and punching attacks.

  • 3-side live locking bolts
  • Hardened steel bolt material
  • Precision-machined lock case
  • Redundant re-locker device
04 / DIGITAL

Digital Locking Options

For clients who prefer electronic access, we offer validated digital lock systems with audit trail, user codes and override key options.

  • Electronic keypad access
  • Audit trail logging
  • Emergency override key
  • Battery backup system
05 / MECHANICAL

Mechanical Locking Options

Traditional mechanical dial and key locks remain popular for their reliability and independence from power sources.

  • Combination dial locks
  • Double-key systems
  • Changeable combination options
  • Proven long-term reliability
06 / PROTECTION

Anti-Drill & Fire Resistance

Additional protection layers include hardened anti-drill plates around the lock area and optional fire-resistant insulation for document protection.

  • Hardened drill-resistant plates
  • Composite barrier materials
  • Fire insulation options
  • Thermal seals on select models

Inspection Stages

Dimensional Accuracy

Panel dimensions, squareness and tolerances checked with precision instruments.

Surface Finish

Functional Testing
